Service Providers accessed through Meld support a wide variety of fiat currencies. You can find a list of supported fiat currencies for onramps and offramps below:

[Supported Fiat Currencies for Onramps](#supported-fiat-currencies-for-onramps)<br />[Supported Fiat Currencies for Offramps](#supported-fiat-currencies-for-offramps)

The `GET /service-providers/properties/fiat-currencies` endpoint can also be used to obtain this information.
